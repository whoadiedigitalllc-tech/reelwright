# Fixes and angle (web)

Sample: the same 397 rows as ../fixes.md (300 App Store reviews of the mobile app, 98 HN comments), filtered for
web, cloud, account and privacy themes. No reviews of CapCut's web editor specifically were collected.
G2/Capterra/Trustpilot (where web users review it) weren't read. So everything below is thin.

## What they hate (web-relevant)

1. **Uploaded content isn't private / terms claim rights over content.** 2-3 reviews, 2 sources (thin).
   - "I don't see a way to make the gallery you upload private. I can't even find a way to delete the gallery." (1★) https://apps.apple.com/us/app/capcut-photo-video-editor/id1500855883?see-all=reviews#review-14798024185
   - "They now claim broad licenses over user content." (HN) https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44790034
2. **Sign-in friction.** A handful of App Store reviews (thin), e.g. Google sign-in stuck on the birth-date screen.
   - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/capcut-photo-video-editor/id1500855883?see-all=reviews#review-14794056344
3. Everything in ../fixes.md (paywalls 47 reviews / 2 sources, bugs 32 / 2) applies to the web editor too: same company, same Pro gating (memory).

## Fix plan (web)

| # | fix | size | status | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| W1 | Files never leave the computer: no upload, nothing to make private | S | **done** (by design) | privacy, 2-3 thin |
| W2 | No sign-in at all | S | **done** | sign-in friction, thin |
| W3 | Say plainly where projects live ("saved in this browser") so clearing site data is no surprise | S | **done** (this step) | data-loss expectation from parity.md (no review evidence) |
| 1-3 | Free, no export lock, local-first (../fixes.md) | S | **done** | paywall 47/2 sources |

## Angle (web)

Recommended (A, adapted): **"A video editor in your browser where every feature is free, and your footage never leaves your computer."**
Evidence: paywalls (47 reviews, 2 sources); privacy of uploads (thin).
